AI Customised News

TKG AI Customised News is a developing digital-news service by TKG AI Hub. It is designed to prepare a clearer daily edition according to the reader’s interests, preferred sources, level of detail and available reading time.

The aim: not simply to provide more news, but to provide more relevant, understandable and useful news.

A Practical Customised-News Journey

The future service is designed to let readers control what they receive, while preserving important Singapore and public-interest news.

1

Choose What Matters to You

Readers will be able to define the news subjects, regions and perspectives that matter most to them—from Singapore affairs and world news to AI, business, football, health, travel and learning.

2

Build a Trusted Daily Edition

TKG News Agency will give priority to authoritative sources, including official agencies, reputable Singapore media, international news agencies and relevant specialist sources.

3

Read at Your Own Pace

Readers may choose a short update, a general daily edition or a more detailed briefing. The future service will respect the reader’s available time.

Three Parts of TKG Daily News

Part 1 — Front Page and Must-Read News

Essential stories of the day, with Singapore given priority. This includes major Singapore developments, public policy, world affairs, security and significant regional events.

Part 2 — Business, Markets, Technology and AI

A focused section for the economy, business, markets, digital transformation, artificial intelligence, data analytics and technology, with Singapore, ASEAN and East Asia developments where relevant.

Part 3 — Sports, Analysis, Culture and Learning

A broader section for football and selected sports, culture, entertainment, travel, educational material and useful watch-later videos or tutorials.

Designed for Different Readers

The General Public in Singapore

A reader may prefer Singapore affairs, household issues, healthcare, world affairs, artificial intelligence developments and football—presented in a clear general-reading style.

The Small-Business Owner

A reader may focus on business, economic developments, markets, artificial intelligence, technology and practical SME-related news.

The Learner or Student

A reader may choose simpler explanations, shorter reading time, selected countries and learning topics such as technology, science, skills and current affairs.

What Makes TKG AI Customised News Different?

  • The reader controls topics, regions, sources, detail level and reading time.
  • Singapore and essential public-interest news remain visible.
  • News is selected for relevance, authority and clarity—not only because it is popular or dramatic.
  • Readers can enjoy a focused reading experience designed to be free from advertisement distractions and interruptions.
  • Traditional news subscribers can gradually broaden or adjust their selected sources to include reputable Singapore and international news agencies.
  • The daily edition is designed to reduce repeated headlines and unnecessary information overload.
  • Important stories will include source links, publication context and clear distinction between facts, summaries and opinion.

Responsible News Use

TKG AI Customised News will summarise and organise news from approved sources. It will not copy full news articles.

Readers will be able to open the original source for further reading. For major or sensitive developments, official and primary sources will be prioritised where available.
