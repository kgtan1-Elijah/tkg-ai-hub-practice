Two Consultation Pathways

1. Use AI to Write and Compile Your Book

For people with a story, knowledge, family history, travel experience, hobby, professional expertise or business idea.

  • Book purpose, reader and chapter plan
  • Useful AI prompts and writing workflow
  • Research, notes and source checking
  • Draft improvement while keeping your own voice
  • Images, captions, layout and final checking

Outcome: A practical plan for developing your book with AI.

2. Publish and Sell Your Book

For self-publishers with a completed or near-completed manuscript.

  • Publishing routes and print-on-demand choices
  • Preparing a manuscript and cover for quotation
  • ISBN and legal-deposit preparation
  • Book synopsis, author profile and promotional material
  • Online, bookstore and personal-network sales approaches

Outcome: A realistic publishing and sales action plan suited to your book, budget and readers.

See printer-quotation comparison checklist ▾

Compare quotations using the same book specification. This makes the final comparison fair and meaningful.

Comparison item What to check
Quantity Number of copies, minimum order and reprint options
Book format Finished size, page count and orientation
Cover Paper type, thickness, colour and finish
Inside pages Paper type, thickness, colour or black-and-white printing
Binding Perfect binding, saddle stitch, hardcover or other method
Proof copy Whether a printed proof or digital proof is included
Delivery Production time, delivery date and delivery charges
Total cost Total quotation, unit cost and any additional charges
